Four unit types worth comparing before you commit

Pick the type before the brand, because the type decides gas line, venting, electric load and floor space.

Gas tank

Familiar and quick to recover. Needs a gas connection, a flue or a vent pipe, and clearance from combustibles.

Electric tank

No exhaust to manage, which suits tight spaces. Needs a dedicated circuit and breaker sized for the unit.

Hybrid heat pump

Pulls heat from the surrounding air, so it wants open space and a condensate drain. It cools the room slightly, which some basements welcome.

Tankless

Wall-mounted and compact. Gas models need larger gas supply and special venting, and electric models need heavy wiring. Details are on our tankless water heater page.

Basements, older houses and where to put the heater

In Blue Island, about 64.7% of homes were built before 1970, with a median build year of 1961. The heater often lives in a basement beside a furnace and a floor drain, with a shutoff that predates modern parts. That layout is workable, but every old valve and union is worth a look while the water is off.

The city also sits on a combined sewer system with a documented history of basement flooding. If your basement has taken on water, raising the unit on a stand is a reasonable question to ask during the walkthrough, and so is a drain pan piped to a safe location.

Relocating the heater to a garage, a utility room or a finished corner changes the pipe runs, venting and drain plan. We price that separately so you can see the difference.

A new water heater goes in under its manufacturer's printed instructions and the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890). Gas models also need the venting and gas connection those instructions describe. Ask Building Safety whether your swap needs a city permit before the install date.

Hiring a plumber: licence, registration and the city's permit desk

Illinois licenses plumbers under the Illinois Plumbing License Law, 225 ILCS 320. You can check any plumber on the Illinois Department of Public Health Plumbing License Verification lookup. Ask for the name and search it before you sign.

Blue Island also requires contractors to register with the city, and plumbing contractors must have their state IDPH licenses on file. Whether your install needs a permit is a question for the City of Blue Island Building Safety Department at City Hall, 13051 Greenwood Avenue, so call them early and ask.

Before install day, clear and confirm these

A little prep saves an hour or more of work.

  • 1Clear a path from the door to the heater, about three feet wide.
  • 2Move stored boxes and tools away from the heater and the furnace.
  • 3Locate your main water shutoff and tell us if it sticks.
  • 4Decide who will be home. An adult must be present to approve changes.
  • 5Collect any model numbers, manuals or warranty paperwork for the old unit.
  • 6Plan for no hot water for several hours, and tell everyone in the home.
