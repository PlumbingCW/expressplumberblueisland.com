What to have ready before you dial

A calm minute of preparation shortens the call. You do not need technical words, only the facts below.

  • 1Your street address and ZIP. Blue Island is 60406. We also serve 60803, 60418, 60445 and 60827.
  • 2What you see, hear or smell. Water on the floor, a gurgling drain, a rotten-egg smell or a cold shower all point to different jobs.
  • 3Where the main shutoff is. Usually near the meter or where the water line enters the basement or utility closet.
  • 4Whether anyone is in danger. Gas smell, standing water near outlets and sewage on the floor change the order of what we tell you.
  • 5How we reach you. Keep your phone free after the call in case the plumber has a question.

If water is moving right now

Do these before or during the call. They cost nothing and limit the damage.

  • Close the main shutoff by turning it clockwise.
  • Open one low faucet to drain what is left in the line.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and the washer.
  • Stay away from wet outlets, cords and the breaker panel.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

Winter calls

In a deep freeze, pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages and crawl spaces are the first to split. The Chicago-area (O'Hare) January average low is near 18.8 F, so a slow tap on a cold morning deserves a call before it becomes a burst. Shut the main valve if a pipe is visibly split, then ring (773) 207-0518.
