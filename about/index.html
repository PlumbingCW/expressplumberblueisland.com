What this company is, and what it is not

Blue Island Express Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 12601 S Western Ave, Blue Island, IL 60406. We take calls 24/7 and send licensed plumbers to homes and small businesses in Blue Island and four nearby towns. That is the whole pitch.

We are not a remodeling firm, and we do not promise work outside plumbing. If a wall needs rebuilding after a pipe repair, we will tell you so and leave the carpentry to someone else.

How we price plumbing work

We quote the job after the plumber has seen it, not before and not by guess over the phone. The number you hear is the number you are deciding on. If the scope changes mid-job, the plumber stops and explains why before adding to it.

We do not publish a dollar menu because pipes are not uniform. A cracked 1960s drain and a modern PVC line do not cost the same to fix.

AccessA pipe in an open basement ceiling costs less to reach than one behind finished tile or in a slab.
Pipe materialCopper, galvanized steel, cast iron and PVC each need different tools, fittings and labor.
Parts and fixturesA common valve costs little. A specialty cartridge or a tank sized for your home adds to the total.
Time of dayNight work needs a plumber awake and a truck stocked. Ask for the price before you agree.
DiagnosisA mystery leak takes longer to trace than a visible split, and the work reflects that.

On insurance: sudden burst damage is the usual covered case. Gradual leaks, wear and sewer backup often need a specific endorsement, so check your policy. Want your real number? Call (773) 207-0518.

How to check any plumber's licence in Illinois

Illinois plumbers are licensed by the Illinois Department of Public Health under the Plumbing License Law, 225 ILCS 320. IDPH runs a Plumbing License Verification lookup online, so you can type a name or number and see whether it is current. Do this for us, and do it for anyone else you hire.

Blue Island also asks plumbing contractors to register with the city, with a current state licence on file. The city says a plumber pulling a permit must show a valid licence, unless the owner-occupant of a single-family home does the work. We are licensed and insured, and we expect to be checked.

Where we work

Our base is the Western Avenue corridor in Blue Island, a city of about 22,500 people and roughly 8,464 occupied homes. Many of those homes are older, with a median build year of 1961, so cast iron drains and aging shutoffs are common.

From there we serve Alsip, Crestwood, Midlothian and Riverdale. The service area page explains how dispatch is organized, and the contact page lists what to have ready when you call.

Plumbing in Blue Island runs under the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890), which the city lists among the codes it follows. Plumbers need a state licence under the Illinois Plumbing License Law (225 ILCS 320), and plumbing contractors register with the city under Ordinance 99-247 with their state licences on file.
