How dispatch works by geography

When you call (773) 207-0518, the dispatcher asks for your street address first. That tells us which of the four towns you are in and how to route a plumber. A leak in Alsip and a leak in Riverdale are handled the same way, but the plumber's trip comes from different directions.

Urgent calls, such as a burst pipe, a sewage backup or a leaking water heater, go to the front of the line at any hour. Lower-risk jobs are booked for a same-day appointment when available. No one can promise an exact arrival time on a stormy night, and we do not.

Check that your address is covered

ZIP codes do not match town lines neatly. A few streets near a boundary sit in a different ZIP from the one you expect. Use this list to confirm before you rely on it.

  • 1Find the ZIP on a utility bill or mailbox. Blue Island is 60406, Alsip 60803, Crestwood 60418, Midlothian 60445 and Riverdale 60827.
  • 2Note the town name on your water bill. Blue Island buys water from Chicago, and so do Alsip and Riverdale. The village on your bill is the town that governs your pipes.
  • 3Call (773) 207-0518 and give your street. The dispatcher confirms coverage during the call.
  • 4Ask about nearby edge cases. If you live near a boundary, say so. We route by address, not by the ZIP alone.

Places near our borders that we mention but do not list

Several small places sit within a couple of miles of Blue Island by straight-line estimate: Calumet Park (about 1.3 miles), Dixmoor (about 1.8), Posen (about 2.0), Robbins (about 1.7) and Merrionette Park. The Morgan Park area of Chicago adjoins Blue Island's northern boundary.

We do not have separate pages for these places. If you live in one, call (773) 207-0518 and ask. The answer depends on your street and the time of day, so we confirm by phone instead of guessing.

Each village sets its own plumbing paperwork, while the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890) is the state code in all four. Blue Island uses section 150.052, Alsip inspects through its Building Department, Crestwood and Midlothian run their own permit pages, and Riverdale uses Inspectional Services. Confirm yours when you call.

Why coverage and flooding often go together here

Parts of the Calumet corridor are flat and drain slowly. The Center for Neighborhood Technology reports 3,362 flood-related insurance claims in Riverdale's ZIP 60827 from 2007 to 2011, and 469 in Blue Island's 60406. That is why many of our calls near the Little Calumet River and the Cal-Sag Channel are about drains and basements.

If heavy rain is in the forecast, check your floor drain and sump pump now. Our pages on sump pump service and sewer line cleaning explain the usual fixes.
