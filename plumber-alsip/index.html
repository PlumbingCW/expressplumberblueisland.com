Slow drains and backups: what the sign points to

Start with the symptom, not the pipe. One slow sink is usually a local clog. Several fixtures misbehaving together means the main line is restricted, and that is a different job.

  • 1Gurgling after a flush. Air is being pulled through a trap because the line downstream is partly blocked.
  • 2Water rising in the basement floor drain when the washer runs. The main drain cannot carry the load, so the lowest opening gives first.
  • 3Slow tub and slow toilet at once. Two fixtures sharing one branch rarely clog on the same day by coincidence.
  • 4A sewer smell near the floor drain on a dry day. That is a trap or line problem, not the weather.
  • 5The same clog returning within weeks. A cable that only punches a hole leaves the pipe wall coated, so it closes up again.

The Metropolitan Water Reclamation District lists Alsip on separate sewers, not combined ones. A backup on a dry day therefore points at your own lateral or the pipe wall, not at a district overload. Our drain cleaning crews start with a cable and a camera to see which it is. When roots or grease have scaled the wall, hydro-jetting with a high-pressure water nozzle scours it instead of drilling through.

Repair or replace: a quick read on common heater problems

A repair is the right call when one part has failed and the tank is sound. When the steel shell leaks, no part swap helps.

What each heater symptom usually means
SymptomLikely causeUsual direction
No hot water at allFailed element, thermostat or pilot assemblyRepair, if the tank is dry

Before we arrive at your Alsip home

Emergency dispatch for Alsip runs around the clock, and a few minutes of prep make the visit faster. Keep the list short and stay safe.

Stop using water

If a drain is backing up, hold off on the washer, dishwasher and showers. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go.

Find the main shutoff

Look near where the water line enters the house, usually by the meter. Turn the valve clockwise if water is spreading.

Cut the heater

For a leaking tank, switch off the power or set gas to pilot, then close the cold valve above the tank.

Clear a path

Move boxes from the floor drain, utility sink and heater so the crew can reach them. Keep children and pets away from wet floors.
