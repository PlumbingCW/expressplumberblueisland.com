Disposal leaking or backed up? Do this first

Never put your hand in the disposal, even if you think the power is off. Use the breaker to cut power to the kitchen circuit if you can reach it safely, and keep the bucket and towels ready for the cabinet floor.

  • Turn off the disposal switch and then the breaker that feeds it. A wall switch alone is not a safe way to work near a jammed unit.
  • Place a bucket under the leak and empty it often. If water drips from the housing, stop using the sink until it is checked.
  • If the sink is backing up, do not run the dishwasher, because it drains through the same disposal connection.
  • Close the small shutoff valves under the sink only if the leak is coming from a supply line and not from the drain side.
  • Keep water away from outlets and power strips under the sink. Then call (773) 207-0518.

Repairing or installing a garbage disposal, step by step

Whether the disposal is jammed or brand new, the same four stages apply.

01

Power off and inspect

We disconnect power, look at the mounting ring, the drain outlet, the dishwasher connection and the wiring or plug.

02

Free, repair or remove

A jam is cleared with a hex wrench from underneath. A dead motor or cracked housing means the unit comes out.

03

Mount and connect

A new disposal is hung from the sink flange, wired or plugged in, and connected to the trap and the dishwasher hose.

04

Test under load

We run water and the unit, check every joint for weeping, and confirm the dishwasher drains.

If a kitchen has no disposal yet, installation needs a switch or outlet in reach, a sink with the standard opening, and room under the basin for the unit.

Repair, replace or leave the disposal out

A disposal is a modest appliance. Choose the option that fits how you use your kitchen.

Repair

Makes sense for a jam, a loose flange or a bad connection. If the unit is only a few years old, repair is usually the better value.

Replace

Best when the housing leaks or the motor burns out. A bigger motor handles more food waste and runs quieter.

Batch-feed model

Runs only when the stopper is in place. Households with small children often prefer it.

Skip it

Some kitchens are better off with a strainer and a compost bin. We can cap the outlet and leave a clean trap.

Keeping a new disposal out of trouble

A few habits cut the repeat calls.

  • 1Run cold water before, during and for about 15 seconds after grinding.
  • 2Keep grease, coffee grounds, rice, pasta and fibrous scraps such as celery and corn husks out of the drain.
  • 3Feed small amounts at a time instead of loading the chamber.
  • 4Grind a few ice cubes now and then to knock off buildup, and never use chemical drain cleaners in the unit.
